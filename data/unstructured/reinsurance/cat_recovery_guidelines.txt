Reinsurance treaty review should evaluate treaty type, attachment point, limit amount, retention, reinsurer share, catastrophe aggregation, recoverable estimates, exhaustion scenarios, and reinstatement terms.

CAT recovery exposure should consider:
- treaty attachment point
- available limit amount
- retained loss
- reinsurer share
- potential recoverable estimate
- exhaustion scenario
- reinstatement terms

Treaties with high CAT aggregation or low attachment points require close recoverable monitoring.
