CAT event assessment should consider event type, region, estimated loss amount, affected claim count, severity level, claims surge, reserve adequacy, catastrophe response procedures, and reinsurance recovery implications.

Operational impact should include:
- claims surge monitoring
- reserve adequacy review
- catastrophe response activation
- staffing impact
- exposure aggregation
- reinsurance recovery implications

High severity CAT events require escalation, reserve monitoring, and coordination with reinsurance teams.
