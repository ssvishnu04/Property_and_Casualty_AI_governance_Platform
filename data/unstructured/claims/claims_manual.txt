Claims Handling Manual

All claims must undergo initial triage within 24 hours of FNOL intake.

Adjusters should validate:
- policy effective dates
- coverage eligibility
- loss cause
- reserve adequacy
- supporting documentation

Large-loss claims exceeding $500,000 require escalation to senior claims management.

Litigation indicators include:
- attorney representation
- disputed liability
- suspected bad faith exposure
- subrogation disputes

Claims involving catastrophe events should follow CAT surge handling procedures.

Reserve reviews are required every 30 days for open commercial claims.
