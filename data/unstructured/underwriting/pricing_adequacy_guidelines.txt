Underwriting review should evaluate catastrophe exposure, total insured value, requested limits, deductible, pricing adequacy, and risk appetite.

Submissions with high catastrophe exposure, high total insured value, or weak pricing adequacy require secondary underwriting review.

Underwriting concerns should include:
- catastrophe exposure
- pricing adequacy
- requested limits
- deductible suitability
- risk appetite alignment
- need for secondary underwriting review
